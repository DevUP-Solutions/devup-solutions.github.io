# Why Your Azure Integration Environment Needs a Watchdog – Before It’s Too Late

In many organizations, the Azure Integration landscape is a living, breathing ecosystem.  
New Logic Apps, Service Bus topics, and API connections appear weekly.  
Changes happen fast — and often without a clear picture of the bigger impact.

On paper, everything looks fine.  
Until… it’s not.

---

## The Hidden Risks Lurking in Azure

If you’re leading an integration or cloud team, you probably know the obvious risks: downtime, security breaches, and compliance failures.  
But here’s the catch — most of these don’t announce themselves.

Common issues we see every week:

- **Misconfigurations** that open security gaps  
- **Idle or forgotten resources** still costing money  
- **Failed Logic Apps** silently breaking business processes  
- **Outdated configurations** not aligned with Microsoft best practices  

The worst part?  
Many teams find out **after** the damage is done.

---

## Why Traditional Monitoring Isn’t Enough

Standard monitoring tools can alert you when something fails — but they rarely show you the *why* or the *what’s next*.  
They don’t provide a holistic, daily view of your environment’s health.

That means small issues stack up over time until they become big, costly problems.
